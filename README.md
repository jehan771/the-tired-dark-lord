## About

A make.fun entry for [The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb): one monologue of exactly 100 words, spoken by a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired. The tiredness lives in the voice, the rhythm and the small deflections — never in a plain statement — and the speaker stays a villain, with a scheme and a history.

## The monologue

> Come in. Mind the skulls — they were a statement once. Now they're in the way. I took the Hollow Marches in nine days. Nine. The bards still sing it. They skip the tithes. The border disputes. Men who call me "my lord" and mean "my problem." I raised the dead because the living asked questions. The dead don't ask. They stand there. Waiting. So take the throne. Take the crown, the sword, all of it. I ask this: when you strike me down, do it before lunch. I have a nap scheduled. For once, I intend to keep it.

**100 words, 512 characters** (513 with the trailing newline) — inside the 1–600 character gate.

Why it should play well in a game:

- **Specific villain, concrete history:** conqueror of the Hollow Marches in nine days, an army of the dead raised because the living asked questions, tithes and border disputes piling up behind the legend.
- **Tiredness felt, not announced:** the pauses ("Waiting."), the deflections (the bards sing the conquest, never the paperwork), the giveaway of throne, crown and sword.
- **Speakable, with a sting:** one breath of dialogue, landing on the last line — a nap, scheduled, and for once kept.
- **Every word earns its place:** 100 words exactly, no filler — the skulls, the tithes, the committee of the dead that never asks, all do double work as setting and as exhaustion.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts   # writes /output/entry.txt (deterministic)
```

Requires Node 22.18 or later. The program asserts the monologue is exactly 100 words, 1–600 characters, and never states the tiredness outright ("tired", "weary", "exhausted", "fatigue", "sleepy" are all rejected at build time).
